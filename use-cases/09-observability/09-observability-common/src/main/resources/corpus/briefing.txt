Aurora Labs company briefing.

Aurora Labs is a research company headquartered in Reykjavik, Iceland. It was founded in 2019 and
employs about 120 people across engineering, research and operations.

The company's flagship product is called Lumen. Lumen is an analytics platform for renewable energy
grids. It integrates with weather forecasting services to predict energy demand and to balance supply
from wind and geothermal sources.

Aurora Labs runs its own data centre in Reykjavik, powered entirely by geothermal energy. The Lumen
platform is offered as a hosted service and is used by grid operators in several northern European
countries.
